\begin{abstract}
We compare four compact convolutional approaches to workout-video
classification: per-frame prediction, late fusion, early fusion, and a 3D CNN.
On the original course splits, per-frame probability averaging reaches
91.7\% validation accuracy, compared with 30.8--49.2\% for the other
models trained from scratch. Transferring the per-frame weights to late
fusion also reaches 91.7\%, showing that training strategy matters in this
comparison. These are preliminary, single-seed validation results on splits
with known subject leakage. Corrected-split, optical-flow, and final-test
experiments remain to be completed.
\end{abstract}
