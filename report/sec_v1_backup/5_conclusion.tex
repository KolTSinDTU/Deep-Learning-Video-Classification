\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Per-frame aggregation achieves the highest original-split validation
accuracy in the cluster runs (90.8\%), followed by late fusion (75.0\%).
Early fusion and the 3D CNN fit the training data less well under the
chosen optimization and stopping settings. These single-seed results do
not isolate architectural effects from training-budget differences, and
subject leakage limits their interpretation. Held-out test evaluation,
the corrected-split comparison, and the contribution of optical flow
remain necessary before drawing conclusions about generalization.
